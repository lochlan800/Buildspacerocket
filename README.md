# 🚀 Space Rocket Builder Game

A fun, interactive HTML5 game where you build rockets, launch them to space, dodge obstacles, and land on planets!

## 🎮 How to Play

### Phase 1: Build Your Rocket
1. Click buttons to add rocket parts:
   - **Fuel Tank** - Gives you fuel to reach higher altitudes
   - **Booster** - Increases your rocket's power for faster launching
   - **Payload** - Extra cargo (adds to complexity)
   - **Heat Shield** - Protects your rocket

2. More parts = more power, but heavier rockets! Find the right balance.
3. Click **LAUNCH!** when you're ready to blast off!

### Phase 2: Fly Through Space
- Use **Arrow Keys** (or tilt your device on mobile) to steer left and right
- Dodge **asteroids** and **debris** floating in space
- Each obstacle you dodge gives you points!
- Your fuel bar depletes as you climb - reach 400+ km altitude to proceed to landing
- Watch out: run out of fuel and you'll need to land!

### Phase 3: Choose Your Planet
- Select a destination to land on:
  - 🌙 **Moon** - Easy, close by
  - 🔴 **Mars** - Red planet, challenging
  - 🟠 **Jupiter** - Giant gas planet
  - 💛 **Saturn** - Ringed beauty

### Phase 4: Land Safely
- Click **LAND!** when your rocket is in the **GREEN ZONE** (safe landing area)
- Time it perfectly for a smooth touchdown!
- Miss the zone? Don't worry - you can crash land and still earn points!
- Successful landings reward you with bonus points based on remaining fuel

## 🎯 Scoring

- **Dodging Obstacles:** 10 points each
- **Successful Landing:** 100 points + fuel bonus
- **Crash Landing:** 30 points
- Your **High Score** is saved automatically!

## 🚀 Tips for Success

1. **Balance your rocket** - More boosters = faster flight, but add some fuel tanks
2. **Stay centered** - The landing zone is in the middle of the screen
3. **Dodge early** - React to obstacles before they hit you
4. **Fuel management** - Better rockets with more fuel get bigger bonuses!
5. **Practice** - Each game generates different obstacle patterns, so it's always fresh!
